## Аннотация

[источник] Soil microbial community plays crucial roles in promoting soil
functions and maintaining soil health. Microbial functional gene abundances are
actively involved in soil processes which supports soil functions and wider soil
health. However, their suitability as indicators to assess soil health is still
debatable. In this study, we sampled soils from a 10-year long-term fertilization
experiment in a wheat-maize cropping system on the North China Plain. The
treatment included no fertilizer (Control), chemical fertilizers only (NPK),
NPK + organic manure, NPK + straw, and NPK + manure + straw. We quantified
seventeen functional genes involved in carbon (cbbL, GH31), nitrogen (nifH,
ureC, chiA, A-amoA, B-amoA, narG, nirK, nirS, norB and nosZ), and phosphorus
(gltA, bpp, phoD, phoC, pqqC)...
