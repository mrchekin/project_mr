from a wide variety of sources. The term glomalin or glomalin-related soil
protein is inaccurate and limits the utility of the method. The extracted protein
pool should be viewed more broadly as a soil health indicator of potentially
available organic N. Increased interest in practical, routine evaluation of soil
health has created a need for rapid and inexpensive indicators that reflect soil
