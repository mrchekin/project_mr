## Аннотация

[источник] Soil health is defined as the capacity of soil to function, within
ecosystem boundaries, to sustain crop and animal productivities, maintain or
enhance environmental sustainability, and improve human health worldwide. In
agro-ecosystems, the soil health can change due to anthropogenic activities, such
as preferred cropping practices and intensive land-use management, which can
further impact soil functions. Previous assessment of soil health in agriculture
mostly relates to soil eco-functions that are integrated with non-biological
properties such as soil nutrients and soil structures. In recent years,
