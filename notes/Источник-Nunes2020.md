## Аннотация

[источник] Tillage intensity affects soil microbiological activity in many ways,
often driven by changes in soil organic C (SOC) content. The magnitude and
direction of those changes, however, depends on inherent (e.g., soil type and
texture), experimental (e.g., study duration and sampling depth) and agronomic
factors (e.g., cropping system and crop residue management). This nationwide
meta-analysis examines published effects of chisel plowing (CP), no-tillage (NT),
and perennial cropping systems (PER) relative to moldboard plow (MP) on seven
soil health indicators: SOC, microbial biomass C (MBC), microbial biomass N (MBN),
soil respiration (Resp), active-C (AC), beta-glucosidase activity (BG) and soil
protein (Prot) within four soil depth increments in 302 studies from throughout
the United States...
